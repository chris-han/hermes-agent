policy source's rules, reviewed absences, conditions, exceptions or conclusions
also occur in this target. schema_adequacy describes that vocabulary's source;
it is not a completeness or authority certificate for the target document.
Use only target source citations for proposed frames and effects. Preserve target
contradictions and missing interpretation as target-source diagnostics. Do not
turn an internal target contradiction into a policy defect or a bidder violation.
